## About

Welcome to Styling in React! In this comprehensive module, we'll explore six diverse and powerful approaches to bringing your React applications to life with style. From the basics of traditional CSS to the dynamic capabilities of modern tools like Sass and CSS Modules, you'll gain the skills to make your apps visually stunning and maintainable in multiple modalities.
